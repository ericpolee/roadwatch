About Me

Hi, I'm Eric Lee, a high school student passionate about using technology to solve real-world problems. I've been coding for several years and love exploring the intersection of machine learning, mobile development, and civic technology.

When I noticed how many road hazards go unreported in my community, I decided to build something that could make a difference. That's how RoadWatch was born—a tool that empowers everyday drivers to contribute to safer roads.

RoadWatch

RoadWatch is a mobile application that uses advanced machine learning to detect road hazards in real-time through your dashcam. Our YOLO Ensemble neural networks analyze footage as you drive, identifying potholes, cracks, debris, animals, malfunctioning traffic lights, and more.

Every detection is automatically logged with GPS coordinates and sent to local public works offices, helping municipalities prioritize repairs based on severity and traffic data. No more waiting for 311 calls—RoadWatch creates a continuous stream of actionable road condition data.

Key Features

  • Real-time pothole, crack, debris, and animal detection using YOLO ML models
  • Easy dashcam integration via Bluetooth or affordable Raspberry Pi solution
  • Interactive mapping with all detected hazards
  • Automated formal reports sent directly to Offices of Public Works
  • Smart severity scoring (1.0-10.0) based on traffic data and repair costs
  • Dashboard for municipalities to track and prioritize repairs
